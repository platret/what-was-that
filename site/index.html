How do I get it on my iPhone?
  1. Download the source from the release page and unzip it on your Mac.
  2. Open WhatWasThat.xcodeproj in Xcode 26 or later.
  3. Select your Apple development team for the app and widget targets. Use your own bundle and App Group identifiers.
  4. Connect your iPhone, enable Developer Mode, select it in Xcode, and press Run.

Widgets require App Groups support for your signing team. The repository includes detailed setup instructions.
